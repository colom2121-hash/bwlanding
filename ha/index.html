Choosing a format

Vial or syringe?

VERSUS vials · 50 / 60 / 100 ml

Flexible dosing

  • Clinic draws the exact volume per area into its own syringes and cannulas
  • Lower cost per millilitre at larger unit sizes
  • Fits practices with established aseptic transfer protocols
VERSUS · Juvelife syringe · 50 ml

Ready to inject

  • Prefilled, so there is no transfer step before treatment
  • Faster chair-side preparation and less handling
  • Suits clinics new to large-volume HA procedures

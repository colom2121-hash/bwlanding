The Science

HA Meets Anthocyanin

Red Filler is Beyondworld's patent-pending breakthrough — a BDDE-crosslinked hyaluronic acid filler fortified with natural anthocyanin antioxidants, engineered for superior longevity and post-procedure skin health.

  • ◈
    ROS Scavenging Anthocyanin neutralises reactive oxygen species generated during injection, reducing inflammation and extending product lifespan.
  • ◈
    MMP Inhibition Suppresses matrix metalloproteinases that degrade native collagen and HA, supporting longer-lasting structural results.
  • ◈
    NRF2 / KEAP1 Pathway Activates endogenous antioxidant response, reinforcing dermal resilience at a cellular level.
  • ◈
    TGF-β Collagen Synthesis Promotes TGF-β-mediated collagen production for biostimulatory afterglow beyond volumisation.
